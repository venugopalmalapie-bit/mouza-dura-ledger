How the figures are worked out
  1. What the partners are owed. For every plot sold, sq ft sold × ₹800 (the 2022 agreed rate). The price MKR got from the buyer does not matter here.
  2. What has been paid. Every amount MKR has handed over to the partners, linked to the plots it was for.
  3. What is still pending. Owed minus paid. Each partner's share is one quarter.

Example: map 33 sold 1,402 sq ft → 1,402 × ₹800 = ₹11,21,600 owed. ₹7,79,944 recorded as paid → ₹3,41,656 pending, ₹85,414 per partner.
